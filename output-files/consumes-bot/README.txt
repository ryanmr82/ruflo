GuildShopSync v2.4 - TBC Guild Consume Shop Price Scanner
=========================================================

INSTALLATION:
1. Extract the "GuildShopSync" folder to your WoW AddOns directory:
   C:\turtlewow\Interface\AddOns\

2. Restart WoW or type /reload in-game

USAGE:
1. Open the Auction House (requires aux addon)
2. Click the "Sync Prices" button in the bottom-right corner
3. Wait for the scan to complete (scans 66 items)
4. Click "Reload UI & Sync" when the popup appears
5. Prices are automatically uploaded to the guild shop website

COMMANDS:
/gss scan   - Start scanning prices
/gss stop   - Stop current scan
/gss debug  - Show debug info

NOTES:
- You must have the Auction House open to scan
- Scan takes 1-2 minutes depending on server response
- Prices update on the website immediately after reload
