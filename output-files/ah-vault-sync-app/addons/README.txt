AH & Vault Sync - WoW Addons
============================

Copy these addon folders to your Turtle WoW Interface/AddOns directory:

1. GuildShopSync - Scans AH prices for consumables
2. GBankExport   - Exports guild bank contents
3. BankAltExport - Exports character bags and personal bank

Installation:
1. Close WoW if it's running
2. Copy all three folders to: [Turtle WoW]\Interface\AddOns\
3. Start WoW and check that addons are enabled

Usage:
- GuildShopSync: Open the AH (requires aux addon), click "Sync Prices" button
- GBankExport: Open the guild bank, it auto-scans
- BankAltExport: Open your personal bank at a banker, it auto-scans

The tray app will automatically detect changes and upload to the website.
