# Turtle WoW Warlock Leveling Guide (Affliction Drain Tank Build)

## Overview
This guide covers the optimal Affliction "Drain Tank" build for leveling a Warlock from 1-60 in Turtle WoW. This build focuses on maximizing sustain through DoTs (Damage over Time) and drain effects, allowing you to grind efficiently with minimal downtime.

## Why Affliction for Leveling?
- **Minimal Downtime**: Trade health for mana with Life Tap, restore health with Drain Life
- **Efficient Multi-Mob Grinding**: DoT multiple enemies simultaneously
- **Self-Sufficient**: Strong sustain without needing food/water
- **Strong in PvP**: Excellent for world PvP encounters while leveling
- **Recent Buffs (2024)**: New talents like Malediction and Soul Siphon make Affliction even stronger

## Talent Build Order (Levels 10-60)

### Levels 10-14 (5 points)
**Improved Corruption 5/5** (Affliction)
- Makes Corruption instant cast
- Essential for every Warlock build
- Allows casting while moving

### Levels 15-19 (5 points)
**Suppression 5/5** (Affliction)
- Reduces chance for Affliction spells to be resisted by 10%
- Critical for consistent damage

### Levels 20-23 (4 points)
**Improved Life Tap 2/2** (Affliction)
- Increases Life Tap effectiveness by 20%
- Core for mana sustainability

**Improved Drain Life 2/2** (Affliction)
- Increases Drain Life effectiveness by 10%
- Essential for health sustain

### Levels 24-26 (3 points)
**Improved Curse of Agony 3/3** (Affliction)
- Increases Curse of Agony damage by 10%
- Important for DoT damage

### Levels 27-29 (3 points)
**Grim Reach 2/2** (Affliction)
- Increases range of Affliction spells by 20%
- Helps with pulling and kiting

**Amplify Curse 1/1** (Affliction)
- Next curse is unresistable
- Useful for tough enemies

### Levels 30-34 (5 points)
**Nightfall 2/2** (Affliction)
- 4% chance for instant Shadow Bolt on corruption tick
- Great for burst damage

**Empowered Corruption 3/3** (Affliction)
- Increases Corruption damage by 36% of spell power
- Massive damage increase

### Levels 35-39 (5 points)
**Siphon Life 1/1** (Affliction)
- Additional DoT that heals you
- Core spell for drain tanking

**Curse of Exhaustion 1/1** (Affliction)
- Slows enemy movement by 30%
- Excellent for kiting

**Improved Siphon Life 3/3** (Affliction)
- Increases Siphon Life damage by 15%

### Levels 40-44 (5 points)
**Shadow Mastery 5/5** (Affliction)
- Increases Shadow damage by 10%
- Huge overall damage boost

### Levels 45-49 (5 points - Switch to Demonology)
**Improved Healthstone 2/2** (Demonology)
- Increases Healthstone healing by 20%

**Improved Voidwalker 3/3** (Demonology)
- Increases Voidwalker effectiveness
- Better pet tanking

### Levels 50-54 (5 points)
**Fel Stamina 5/5** (Demonology)
- Increases pet and your stamina
- More survivability

### Levels 55-59 (5 points)
**Unholy Power 5/5** (Demonology)
- Increases pet damage by 20%
- OR continue in Affliction for Dark Pact

### Level 60
**Dark Pact 1/1** (Affliction) or **Demonic Sacrifice 1/1** (Demonology)

## Attack Rotation

### Single Target (Standard)
1. **Send in Pet** (Voidwalker for tanking)
2. **Curse of Agony** (or Curse of Shadow for more damage)
3. **Corruption** (instant cast while approaching)
4. **Siphon Life** (once available at level 30)
5. **Immolate** (optional, for extra damage)
6. **Drain Life** or **Drain Soul** (filler/finishing)
   - Drain Soul is better than Shadow Bolt as filler
   - Provides soul shards for pets/healthstones

### Multi-Mob Grinding (3-5 enemies)
1. **Corruption** on all targets
2. **Curse of Agony** on all targets
3. **Siphon Life** on all targets
4. **Tab through and refresh DoTs**
5. **Drain Life** lowest health enemy
6. **Life Tap** as needed for mana

### Elite/Boss Rotation
1. **Amplify Curse** + **Curse of Shadow**
2. **Corruption**
3. **Siphon Life**
4. **Immolate**
5. **Shadow Bolt** spam (if Nightfall procs)
6. **Drain Life** to maintain health
7. **Refresh DoTs** before they expire

## Pet Management

### Voidwalker (Primary Leveling Pet)
- Always use while leveling for superior tanking
- **Torment** should be on auto-cast (right-click ability)
- Get new Grimoire ranks ASAP
- Use **Sacrifice** only in emergencies

### Succubus (Alternative for Humanoids)
- Higher DPS than Voidwalker
- **Seduction** useful for CC
- Good for dungeon groups

### Imp (Dungeons/Groups)
- **Blood Pact** provides stamina buff
- Stays at range, safer in dungeons
- **Fire Shield** for tank (if specced)

## Key Consumables & Items

### Essential
- **Healthstones**: Always have max rank ready
- **Soul Shards**: Keep 10-15 minimum
- **Water**: For drinking between pulls (early levels)

### Recommended
- **Minor Healing Potions** (levels 1-20)
- **Lesser Healing Potions** (levels 20-35)
- **Demon Armor** (always active while leveling)
- **First Aid**: Learn for emergency healing

## Leveling Tips

### General Strategy
1. **Always pull with Corruption** - it's instant and you can cast while moving
2. **Life Tap aggressively** - your health regenerates faster than mana
3. **DoT and move** - apply DoTs then move to next mob while first dies
4. **Drain tank multiple mobs** - with good gear, handle 3-5 mobs at once
5. **Keep Voidwalker healthy** - dismiss and resummon if needed

### Stat Priority
1. **Spell Power** (Shadow damage especially)
2. **Stamina** (more health = more Life Tap)
3. **Intellect** (larger mana pool)
4. **Spirit** (health regen between fights)

### Important Quests
- Class quests for demon summons (Imp, Voidwalker, Succubus, Felhunter)
- Dreadsteed quest at 60 (epic mount)
- Any quests rewarding +Shadow damage gear

## Turtle WoW Specific Changes (2024-2025)

### New Affliction Talents
- **Malediction**: Increases periodic shadow damage
- **Soul Siphon**: Enhanced soul shard generation
- **Dark Harvest**: New capstone ability
- **Improved Debilitating Curses**: Better curse effects
- **Resilient Shadows**: Damage reduction
- **Rapid Deterioration**: Faster DoT ticks

### Updated Abilities
- **Drain Soul**: Now better than Shadow Bolt as filler
- **Curse of Agony**: Improved scaling
- **Shadow Mastery**: Available earlier, very powerful by level 40
