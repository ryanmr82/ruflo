📊 WHAT'S NEW:

A new "Player Analysis" tab that shows:
✓ Who used which consumables (food, elixirs, flasks)
✓ Who died and how many times
✓ Who cast dispels (Remove Curse, Cure Disease, etc.)
✓ Who cast protective buffs (Fear Ward, etc.)


⚡ HOW TO USE (3 CLICKS):
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Launch: start_web_app.bat
2. Click: "📊 Player Analysis" tab
3. Select: Your processed raid log (e.g., TurtLog-MC-2025-10-20-Ragnaros.txt)
4. Click: "Analyze Player Behavior"
5. Done! View the results.

  Real-time search as you type
