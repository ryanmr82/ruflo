# Consumables Research - Vanilla WoW 1.12 / Turtle WoW

## Categorization Rules (Based on Research)

### 1. Elixirs
**Duration**: 1 hour (60 minutes)
**Stacking**: In Vanilla 1.12, there is NO battle/guardian split. That came in TBC.
**Examples**:
- Elixir of the Mongoose (+25 Agility, +2% Crit)
- Elixir of Giants (+25 Strength)
- Elixir of Greater Agility (+25 Agility)
- Elixir of Fortitude (+120 Health)
- Greater Arcane Elixir (+35 Spell Damage)
- Elixir of Shadow Power (+40 Shadow Damage, 30 min)
- Elixir of Greater Firepower (+40 Fire Damage, 30 min)
- Elixir of Superior Defense
- Dreamshard Elixir (+2% Spell Crit, +15 Spell Power)
- Elixir of Brute Force (+18 STR, +18 STA)
- Gift of Arthas (+10 Shadow Damage, -5 Spirit, 30 min)

**NOTE**: Winterfall Firewater is technically listed as a "battle elixir" in databases

### 2. Potions
**Duration**: Instant or short-term (varies: 20 seconds to 30 minutes)
**Types**:
- **Healing/Mana**: Major Healing Potion, Major Mana Potion, etc.
- **Rage**: Mighty Rage Potion (+45-75 Rage instant)
- **Protection**: Greater Fire/Frost/Arcane/Nature/Shadow Protection Potion (resistance buffs)
- **Combat**: Mageblood Potion (+12 MP5, 20 sec), Thistle Tea (Rogue energy)
- **Utility**: Limited Invulnerability Potion, Free Action Potion, Swiftness Potion
- **Troll's Blood**: Major Troll's Blood Potion (HoT effect)
- **Stoneshield**: Greater Stoneshield Potion (+2000 Armor, 90 sec)

### 3. Flasks
**Duration**: 2 hours (120 minutes)
**Special**: Persists through death
**Count**: Only 4 in Vanilla
- Flask of Supreme Power (+150 Spell Damage)
- Flask of the Titans (+400 Health)
- Flask of Distilled Wisdom (+2000 Mana)
- Flask of Chromatic Resistance (+25 All Resistances)

### 4. Food Buffs
**Duration**: 10-15 minutes
**Examples**:
- Danonzos Tel'Abim Medley (+2% Haste, 15 min) - Turtle WoW custom
- Danonzos Tel'Abim Delight (+15 STA/SPI, 15 min) - Turtle WoW custom
- Danonzos Tel'Abim Surprise (+12 STA/SPI, 15 min) - Turtle WoW custom
- Dirge's Kickin' Chimaerok Chops (+25 Stamina, 1 hour)
- Blessed Sunfruit (+10 Strength, 10 min)
- Grilled Squid (+10 Agility, 10 min)
- Nightfin Soup (+8 MP5, 10 min)
- Smoked Desert Dumplings (+20 Strength, 15 min)
- Rumsey Rum Black Label (+ Stamina, 15 min)
- Conjured food/water (mage conjured)

### 5. Jujus / Zanza
**Source**: Zul'Gurub consumables
**Duration**: 10-30 minutes
**Examples**:
- Juju Might (+40 Attack Power, 20 min)
- Juju Power (+30 Strength, 1800 sec = 30 min)
- Juju Flurry (+3% Attack Speed, 20 sec)
- Juju Ember (Fire resistance)
- Spirit of Zanza (+50 Spirit, 10 min)
- Ground Scorpok Assay (+25 Agility, 1 hour) - **Actually an elixir!**

### 6. Scrolls
**Duration**: 30 minutes
**Examples**:
- Scroll of Agility IV (+17 Agility)
- Scroll of Strength IV (+17 Strength)
- Scroll of Intellect IV (+17 Intellect)
- Scroll of Protection IV (+300 Armor)

### 7. Weapon Enhancements
**Duration**: 30 minutes to 1 hour
**Types**:
- **Oils** (Casters): Brilliant Wizard Oil (+36 Spell Damage, +1% Spell Crit), Brilliant Mana Oil (+25 Healing, +12 MP5), Lesser Wizard Oil
- **Sharpening Stones** (Melee): Elemental Sharpening Stone (+2% Crit), Dense Sharpening Stone (+8 Weapon Damage), Consecrated Sharpening Stone (+100 AP vs Undead)
- **Poisons** (Rogues): Deadly Poison, Instant Poison, Wound Poison

### 8. World Buffs
**Duration**: 1-2 hours
**Source**: One-time drops from world events/bosses
**Examples**:
- Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer (+140 AP, +5% Crit, +10% Spell Crit, 2 hours)
- Warchief's Blessing (+300 Health, +15% Melee Haste, 2 hours)
- Spirit of Zandalar (+15% Stats, +10% Movement, 2 hours)
- Songflower Serenade (+15 All Stats, +5% Crit, 1 hour)
- Fengus' Ferocity (+200 AP, 2 hours) - Dire Maul
- Mol'dar's Moxie (+15% Stamina, 2 hours) - Dire Maul
- Slip'kik's Savvy (+3% Spell Crit, 2 hours) - Dire Maul
- Sayge's Dark Fortune (+10% Damage, 2 hours) - Darkmoon Faire

### 9. Misc Consumables
**Examples**:
- Demonic Rune (Instant mana restore, life cost)
- Dark Rune (Instant mana restore)
- Goblin Sapper Charge (AoE damage bomb)
- Combat Healing/Mana Potion (In-combat usable)
- Rage of Ages (ROIDS - +25 Agility, +60 Strength, 15 sec)
- Dreamtonic (Unknown - Turtle WoW custom?)

### 10. NOT CONSUMABLES (Passive Buffs)
**These are NOT player-used items - EXCLUDE from tracking:**
- Windfury Totem (Shaman drops totem, nearby melee get buff automatically)
- Mana Spring Totem (Shaman drops totem, party gets mana regen automatically)
- Strength of Earth Totem
- Grace of Air Totem
- Tranquil Air Totem

**Rule**: If it says "gains X from PlayerName's Y" then it's a PASSIVE BUFF, not a consumable!
