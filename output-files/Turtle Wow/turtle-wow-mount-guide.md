## Understanding Turtle WoW's Mount System

In Turtle WoW, mounts work differently than in vanilla WoW:
- **You can use mounts from level 1** without any training required
- Mount speed scales with your character level until you train riding skills
- All mounts stay active in water (you don't dismount)

### Key Differences from Vanilla WoW
- No level requirement to use a mount
- Speed increases gradually with your level
- Mount choice is purely cosmetic (all mounts same speed at same level)
- Exception: Rank 11 PVP mounts are always +100% speed

## Rental Mounts (50 Copper)

### How Rental Mounts Work
- **Cost:** 50 copper per rental
- **Duration:** Lasts as long as you stay mounted
- **Limitation:** Mount vanishes when you dismount
- **Availability:** Every minor civilized town has rental mounts

### Known Rental Locations
- **Goldshire:** Two horses available for rental (confirmed)
- **Other Towns:** Rental mounts are available in various minor civilized towns across both factions

### Important Notes
- Perfect for short trips when you don't want to walk
- Very affordable at only 50 copper
- Cannot be "stored" - disappears immediately upon dismounting
- Developer plans exist to expand rental locations to more areas

## Getting Your First Permanent Mount at Level 18

### The Turtle Mount Quest: "Torta's Egg"

This is the earliest permanent mount you can obtain in Turtle WoW - the **Swift Riding Turtle**.

#### Requirements
- **Minimum Level:** 18 (Alliance can do safely at 18, Horde recommended level 20+ due to travel dangers)
- **Time to Complete:** 30-60 minutes
- **Location:** Darkmoon Faire

#### Step-by-Step Guide

##### Step 1: Find the Darkmoon Faire
The Darkmoon Faire alternates locations weekly:
- **Alliance Week:** South of Goldshire Inn in Elwynn Forest
- **Horde Week:** Southwest of Thunder Bluff in Mulgore
- **Schedule:** Open every day except Wednesday (unpacking day)
- **Location Switch:** Changes between Alliance/Horde on Saturday night

##### Step 2: Accept the Quest
1. Enter the Darkmoon Faire
2. Find **Silas Darkmoon** (a gnome NPC)
3. Accept the quest **"Torta's Egg"**

##### Step 3: Travel to Zoram Strand
1. Travel to **Ashenvale**
2. Go to **Zoram Strand** on the western shore
3. This is where you'll find the Naga enemies

##### Step 4: Kill Nagas for the Egg
Kill the following Naga enemies until **Torta's Egg** drops:
- Wrathtail Sea Witch
- Wrathtail Sorceress
- Wrathtail Razortail
- Wrathtail Wave Rider

##### Step 5: Return and Complete
1. Return to the Darkmoon Faire
2. Turn in the quest to Silas Darkmoon
3. Receive your **Swift Riding Turtle** mount!

### Cross-Faction Darkmoon Faire Access

#### Alliance to Mulgore (Horde DMF):
1. Wetlands' Menethil Harbor → Boat to Theramore
2. Through Dustwallow Marsh → The Barrens
3. The Barrens → Mulgore

#### Horde to Goldshire (Alliance DMF):
1. Orgrimmar Southeast Zeppelin → Grom'gol (Stranglethorn Vale)
2. Stranglethorn → Duskwood
3. Duskwood → Raven Hill → Elwynn Forest
4. Elwynn Forest → Goldshire

### Special Properties of the Turtle Mount
- **Water Speed Bonus:** Goes 10% faster in water than other mounts
- **Never Dismounts in Water:** Like all Turtle WoW mounts
- **Speed Scales:** Adapts to your level or riding skill

## Mount Speed Calculations

### Before Riding Training (Levels 1-39)
Mount speed bonus = **(Your Level ÷ 2)%**

#### Examples:
- **Level 1:** +0.5% movement speed
- **Level 10:** +5% movement speed
- **Level 18:** +9% movement speed
- **Level 20:** +10% movement speed
- **Level 28:** +14% movement speed
- **Level 34:** +17% movement speed
- **Level 40 (no training):** +20% movement speed

### After Riding Training
- **Apprentice Riding (Level 40):** Flat +60% speed (all mounts)
- **Journeyman Riding (Level 60):** Flat +100% speed (all mounts)

## Level 40 Riding Training

### Training Costs
- **First Mount + Training:** Expensive (traditional vanilla pricing)
- **Reputation Discounts:**
  - 10% off with Honored reputation
  - Additional 10% off with PVP Rank 3
- **Subsequent Mounts:** Much cheaper after first purchase

### Where to Train (Horde)
- **Orgrimmar:** Wolf trainers
- **Thunder Bluff:** Kodo trainers
- **Undercity:** Undead horse trainers

### Important Notes
- Your turtle mount will automatically upgrade to +60% speed after training
- All mounts become +60% speed regardless of type
- Epic riding (Journeyman) available at level 60 for +100% speed

## Tips and Recommendations

### For Your Level 16 Undead Warlock
1. **Immediate Option:** Use 50 copper rental mounts for short trips
2. **Level 18 Goal:** Get the free turtle mount from Darkmoon Faire
3. **Level 40 Goal:** Train Apprentice Riding for full +60% speed

### General Mount Tips
1. The turtle mount at level 18 is essentially free (just quest time)
2. Even at low levels, any mount speed is better than walking
3. Mount speed in water + swim speed gear can stack
4. All mounts are cosmetic choices (except Rank 11 PVP mounts)

### Inventory Management
- Consider getting a mount bag if you collect multiple mounts
- Mounts take up bag space in vanilla/Turtle WoW

## Summary

**Rental mounts** provide immediate relief for 50 copper but disappear when dismounted. The **Turtle Mount quest at level 18** gives you a permanent mount that scales with your level - this is your best option before level 40. At level 40, invest in **Apprentice Riding** to get the full +60% speed boost on all mounts.

For a level 16 character, your immediate goal should be reaching level 18 and completing the Darkmoon Faire turtle quest for your first permanent mount!
