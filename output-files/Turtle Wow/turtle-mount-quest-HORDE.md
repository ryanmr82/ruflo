mm# How to Get Your Permanent Turtle Mount at Level 18 - HORDE

## Quick Facts
- **Mount Name:** Swift Riding Turtle
- **Required Level:** 18 minimum (20+ recommended for Horde)
- **Cost:** FREE (just quest time)
- **Time to Complete:** 30-60 minutes
- **This is a PERMANENT mount** that scales with your level

---

## Step 1: When & Where to Find Darkmoon Faire

### Darkmoon Faire Schedule
- **Mulgore Week (EASY):** Southwest of Thunder Bluff
- **Goldshire Week (HARD):** South of Goldshire Inn in Elwynn Forest
- **Switches:** Every Saturday night
- **Closed:** Wednesdays only (unpacking)

### For Horde Players:
- **WAIT for Mulgore week if possible** - much safer
- If you go during Goldshire week, you risk Alliance ganking

---

## Step 2: Accept the Quest

1. Go to Darkmoon Faire
2. Find **Silas Darkmoon** (gnome NPC)
3. Accept quest: **"Torta's Egg"**

---

## Step 3: Get to Zoram Strand in Ashenvale

**Location:** Western shore of Ashenvale (Zoram Strand)

**Route from Orgrimmar:**
1. Exit Orgrimmar to Durotar
2. Take road south to The Barrens
3. Follow road west through Barrens to Ashenvale
4. Head to western coast (Zoram Strand)

**WARNING:** Ashenvale is contested - watch for Alliance

---

## Step 4: Kill Nagas for Torta's Egg

**Kill these Nagas until egg drops:**
- Wrathtail Sea Witch
- Wrathtail Sorceress
- Wrathtail Razortail
- Wrathtail Wave Rider

The egg is a quest drop - keep killing until it drops.

---

## Step 5: Return & Get Your Mount

1. Return to Darkmoon Faire (either location works)
2. Turn in to Silas Darkmoon
3. **You get the Swift Riding Turtle mount!**

---

## Mount Speed at Your Level

- **Level 16 (you now):** +8% speed
- **Level 18 (quest available):** +9% speed
- **Level 20:** +10% speed
- **Level 30:** +15% speed
- **Level 40:** +20% speed (or 60% if you train riding)

Formula: Your level ÷ 2 = speed % bonus

---

## Tips for Success

1. **Best Strategy:** Wait for DMF in Mulgore (Horde territory)
2. **Bring:** Health potions, food/water
3. **Consider:** Bringing friends for Ashenvale safety
4. **Time:** 30-60 minutes total
5. **Egg Drop:** Keep killing nagas, it will drop

## This Mount is Permanent!
Once you complete this quest, you have a mount forever. It gets faster as you level up. Even at +9% speed at level 18, it's better than walking!
